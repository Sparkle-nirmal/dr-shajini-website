About

Dr. Shajini is a paediatric surgeon and paediatric urologist trained across some of Tamil Nadu's leading government medical institutions. Her practice focuses on reconstructive paediatric urology, neonatal surgery, and laparoscopic paediatric general surgery — with a particular commitment to making surgical care as gentle as possible for young patients and their families.

She completed her Fellowship in Paediatric Urology at Sri Ramachandra Institute of Higher Education & Research, Chennai, following her M.Ch. in Paediatric Surgery at Madras Medical College, where she was awarded University Gold Medalist and Best Outgoing Student.
